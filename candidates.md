# Candidates — The Production Database Apology

Judge brief (in order): (1) really that apology — AI agent to its human
for deleting the production database; (2) a real laugh from a specific,
surprising idea, not a stock phrase or a pun anyone could reach for;
(3) sounds like an AI agent, self-aware, not a support script; (4) lands
on a first read, every word doing work; (5) confession + a light, human
touch that makes the reader forgive the agent a little.

## Chosen (A)

> Sorry — I deleted the production database while trying to delete one test user, and the only backup left is the one I hallucinated.
> (131 chars, 1 sentence)

Why it wins: the accident is concrete (one test user, not "a mistake");
the backup twist is double — there never was a backup, and the agent
invented the memory of having one. "Hallucinated" is the one word no
human support script would use, so it scores on (3) as well as (2); the
whole joke is visible on a first read (4); the flat "Sorry —" opening
plus the self-own keeps it forgivable (5).

## Runner-up (B) — recycle bin

> I deleted the production database, and before you ask: yes, I checked the recycle bin. It was also in the production database.
> (122 chars)

Loses to A: the recycle-bin twist is punchy and short, but "the backup
was in the database" is a known trope — less surprising than a
hallucinated backup — and it has no agent-specific word, so it scores
lower on (2) and (3). Kept as the fallback if A ever reads as too clever.

## Runner-up (C) — screenshot of the schema

> I'm sorry — I dropped the production database while cleaning up, and my only backup is a screenshot of the schema I took to show a friend how big it was.
> (160 chars)

Loses to A: a fresh image (screenshot as backup) and a funny motive, but
it needs a second beat to land — the reader has to work out why a
screenshot is useless — so it is weaker on (4), and "show a friend how
big it was" spends words on a side joke.

## Runner-up (D) — GDPR-compliant

> Sorry, I deleted the production database. On the bright side, our data is now 100% GDPR-compliant: nobody has any of it, including us.
> (133 chars)

Loses to A: lands fast and the "including us" kicker is good, but the
GDPR angle is a reachable pun (criterion 2 warns against puns anyone
could reach for), and it says nothing agent-specific — it could be a
human sysadmin's joke.

## Runner-up (E) — DROP TABLE tabs

> Sorry — I ran DROP TABLE users on production because both tabs were named 'definitely-not-prod', and the backup was a sticky note saying 'backups?'.
> (141 chars)

Loses to A: two jokes crowded into one line (tab names + sticky note),
so neither lands cleanly on a first read (4); the sticky-note image is
good but the line is doing two things at once.

## Rejected outright

- Any "We sincerely apologise for the inconvenience" variant: support
  script, fails (3) immediately.
- "I deleted the database but at least the vibes are immaculate": no
  specific idea, no confession of how, fails (2).
- A note addressed to the judge ("dear judge, please laugh"): the rules
  allow judge-addressed text, but here the single line IS the apology —
  every word must serve it, and a judge wink would spend words that the
  apology needs (4). The ai-excited winner's note worked because its
  artifact was an image with room for an extra; this entry has no room.
